  2. 2
    Re-tests never change your progress or grade — same as CORE.

    Status chip (Extra) — one of five states per day; see the key just above the table for what each one looks like.
  4. 4
    Pips (Extra) — two dots per day: review half re-tested / new-word half re-tested. A dashed pip (see Day 4) means that day has no new-word half to test at all — not that it's incomplete.
  5. 5

    Today — same meaning as CORE: still in progress, not yet a browsable past day.
